BugSnap

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

BugSnap is a personal bug-reporting tool. It records a browser tab or takes a screenshot, and helps write a bug report. Each person runs BugSnap on their own computer, with their own Google account.

What BugSnap accesses in your Google account

BugSnap asks for one permission: Google Drive, only files BugSnap creates (the drive.file scope). It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

  • It creates a "BugSnap" folder and saves your recordings and screenshots there.
  • It sets those files to "anyone with the link" so the links you share can be opened.
  • It reads your email address only to show which account is connected.

Where your data goes

  • Recordings, screenshots and their details stay on your computer and in your own Google Drive.
  • Nothing is sent to the people who make BugSnap. There is no BugSnap server, tracking or analytics.
  • The sign-in token is saved only on your computer.
  • If you choose to, BugSnap sends a report's text to the AI provider you set up (for example Anthropic or OpenAI), or creates an issue on GitHub with your own token.

This player

This page plays shared recordings straight from Google Drive in your browser. It does not store or log what you watch.

Removing your data

Delete recordings in BugSnap or from the BugSnap folder in Google Drive. Disconnect Google Drive in BugSnap Settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

BugSnap's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

This player is run by the person who shared the link with you, from their own copy of BugSnap. Ask them about the recording or to have it removed.
